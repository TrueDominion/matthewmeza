## The crossing

Financial governance architecture designed and deployed from scratch inside high-accountability environments, where the constraint is not margin optimization but mission integrity, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder trust.

## What changed

- **Zero-based budgeting.** Every line justified from the ground up rather than rolled forward.
- **Real-time variance reporting.** Variance architecture that surfaces deviations while they can still be acted on.
- **Board-level accountability.** IFRS-compliant reporting, capital allocation discipline, and treasury oversight built where none existed.
- **Revenue cycle integrity.** End-to-end RCM across claims, payer contracts, and denial management, with IFRS 9 / ECL-compliant receivables and significant reductions in AR days and collection risk exposure.

## What transferred

- Zero-based budgeting
- Variance governance
- Outcome-oriented design
