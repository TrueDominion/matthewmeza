## The crossing

Amazon-scale operational discipline ($200M+ in inventory, 125+ associates, robotics fulfillment at peak throughput) translated into hospital supply chain, procurement reform, and ERP implementation built from zero.

## What changed

- **Procurement cycle time cut by a third.** A continuous improvement framework compressed hospital supply chain lead time by one-third.
- **Vendor and inventory governance.** Vendor governance, formulary management, and inventory optimization run with the same metric discipline as a fulfillment network.
- **ERP from selection to stability.** Full implementation ownership, from system selection and vendor governance through go-live, post-implementation stability, and integration remediation, built with internal talent at every layer.

## What transferred

- Continuous improvement systems
- Accountability frameworks
- Data-driven decisions
